Bark Blocks Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Bark Blocks is a free dog-themed block puzzle game for iPhone made by Blake Dunham ("we"). This policy explains what information is collected when you play and how it is used.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, or contact details.
  • Your game progress, stats, and custom dog stay on your device. We do not receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads.
  • Players under 13 only get child-safe, non-personalized ads and are never tracked.

Information that stays on your device

Your scores, bones, unlocked items, settings, the dog you create, the birth year you enter for the age check, and in-game statistics are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this information.

If you use "Match a photo of your dog", the photo you pick is used only on your device to choose colors. The photo is not saved or uploaded; only the colors you pick are kept.

Information collected by our advertising partner

Bark Blocks uses Google AdMob to show ads, which keeps the game free. The AdMob software in the app may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking when the app asks
  • Approximate location, estimated from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure how they perform, prevent fraud, and, if you allow it, show ads more relevant to you. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: You can allow or deny tracking when Bark Blocks asks, and change it any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland): Where required by law, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool.
  • Delete your data: Deleting the app removes all game data stored on your device.

Children

Bark Blocks asks for the player's birth year before showing ads. Players under 13 are never shown the tracking request, and their ads are marked as child-directed, so they are non-personalized and limited to content suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child's information has been collected, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
